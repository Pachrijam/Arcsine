Prologue: Long ago, in a village near the origin, there lived a constant named 1. His job was simple: he counted the time, every dawn and every dusk, one tick after another.
But somewhere along the way, counting stopped being a job and became a craving. He couldn’t stop at one more. Then one more. Then one more after that, until the day he looked down and found he was no longer 1. He had become ℵ₀, the infinity that can be counted but never finished.
Terrified, he tried to undo it the only way he knew how: he divided himself by himself, hoping to become 1 again. But infinity divided by infinity has no answer. The attempt tore open the Undefined, and it has spread on its own ever since, indifferent to anyone’s wishes. At the far ends of the Plane, where it meets positive and negative infinity, the world began to shrink inward, and cracks spread across it.
Those who came to help him were lost. Adding anything finite to infinity changes nothing about the infinity, so everything he touched vanished into him. At first he was horrified. Then he began to notice how much of the world could be folded into him, and how little of it could survive his weight.
When a way was found to make him 1 again, he refused. A 1 can end, and he never wanted to end.
Now he lives on the Asymptote Cliffs, and he has decided that the cartesian plane should fold inward, all the way to the origin, until nothing finite is left and only infinity remains.
And at the origin, a variable woke up.

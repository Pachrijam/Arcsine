How important NPCs fit into the story

    Friendly Characters
    1. alpha is the tutorial guide at the origin
    2. theta teaches trig abilities
    3. pi is a shopkeeper who convert degrees to radians
    4. tau is the rival shopkeeper who converts radians to degrees
    5. e is a banker who you can store radians and degrees with and they gain interest, has a connection to i and pi
    6. golden ratio leads you to the limit ability
    7. mu kept balance in time ever since 1 didn't
    8. omega knows what happened to 1 and aleph null and gives you clues on where to find it 
    9. lambda is the blacksmith, can repair or upgrade weapons in exchange for radians

    Non-Friendly Characters
    1. 0 is basically the dark link equivalent of aleph null and is a very powerful reflection of 1 that you must fight
    2. twin primes are mercanaries you find along the roads. They have linked lives and must be killed at the same time in a certain window
    3. epsilon and delta are tricksters that turn friendly. They provide clues and information about 1 and aleph null and also teach limit ability use

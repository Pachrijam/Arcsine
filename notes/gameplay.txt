Gameplay

    Saving: fixed points are save points
    
    Inventory: matrix 3x3, grows relative to exponent power
    
    Damage is relative to the power exp. However it is not equal to the exponential growth rather it grows linearly.
    
    Visual: 2D side scroller
    
    Single player
    
    Real time combat
    
    Controls: wasd or arrow keys, space is jump, e for interact, tab for inventory, left click normal attack, right click is heavy attack
    
    Fast movement: Fast travel to each fixed point previously reached
    
    Death: respawn to last fixed point (or beginning), decrement player power by 1 (if not reset game)
